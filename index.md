# LunarCal Privacy Policy

Effective 20 November 2026

LunarCal is a calendar app for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

## In short

LunarCal doesn't collect any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and its developer never receives your calendar, your reminders or your location.

## Your calendar, reminders and birthdays

With your permission, LunarCal reads and saves events, reminders and birthdays through iOS. They stay in Calendar and Reminders, on your devices and in your own iCloud account, and are never sent to the developer.

## What LunarCal keeps

Your settings stay on your device. Your lunar (农历) repeating events, alert snoozes and alert settings are kept in your private iCloud storage, so LunarCal on your other devices can use them. Only you can reach your private iCloud data; the developer can't.

## Location and weather

With your permission, LunarCal uses your location to get the weather forecast from Apple Weather and the name of your town from Apple Maps. Your location goes to Apple only to answer each request. LunarCal doesn't store it or share it with anyone.

## Subscriptions

LunarCal Pro is sold and managed by Apple through the App Store. LunarCal never sees your payment details; Apple only tells it whether a subscription is active.

## Your choices

You can turn off LunarCal's access to your calendars, reminders, location or notifications at any time in the Settings app. Deleting LunarCal removes everything it keeps on your device. To remove what it keeps in iCloud too, delete LunarCal's data from your iCloud storage in the Settings app.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new effective date.
