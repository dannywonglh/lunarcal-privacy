# LunarCal Support

LunarCal is a calendar app for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch that shows lunar (农历) dates or the Moon's phases beside your events and reminders.

## Contact

Email apps.bydannywong@gmail.com with any question, problem or idea. It helps to say which device you're using, its iOS or watchOS version, and what happened.

## Calendar and reminders

### Why don't I see my events or reminders?

LunarCal needs full access to them. In the Settings app, go to Apps → LunarCal, set Calendars to Full Access and turn on Reminders. Then, in LunarCal's own Settings (the gear at the top right), check that the calendars and reminder lists you want are switched on.

### How do I show lunar dates or the Moon's phases?

In LunarCal's Settings, turn on Lunar Date and choose Chinese Lunar Date or Moon Phase. Turn it off to see Gregorian dates only.

### How do I add something that repeats by the lunar calendar, like a lunar birthday?

With Lunar Date set to Chinese Lunar Date, tap +, turn on Use Lunar Calendar, choose the lunar date, and set Repeat to Every lunar year or Every lunar month. LunarCal saves the repeats, up to 2099, in a calendar (or reminder list) called Lunar.

### How do I jump to a date?

Touch and hold the month at the top to open Go to…, then pick the date. While Lunar Date shows Chinese lunar dates, you can pick a lunar date too.

## Weather and LunarCal Pro

### Why is there no weather?

LunarCal needs your location for the forecast. In the Settings app, go to Apps → LunarCal → Location and choose While Using the App. To fetch the weather again straight away, touch and hold today's weather in the agenda and tap Update Weather Now.

### What does LunarCal Pro include?

The weather for each of the next 10 days, in the app and in the Agenda widget. Without Pro, LunarCal shows today's weather only. Everything else in LunarCal is free.

### I've subscribed. Why isn't Pro on?

Pro turns on by itself on every device signed in to the App Store with the same Apple Account. If it hasn't, open LunarCal's Settings, tap LunarCal Pro, then tap Restore Subscription.

### How do I cancel or change my plan?

In LunarCal's Settings, tap Manage Subscription. Or, in the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions. After you cancel, Pro stays on until the end of the period you've paid for.

### How do I get a refund?

Apple handles all payments, so refunds are requested from Apple at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

## Alerts

### Why do I get two notifications for one event?

With LunarCal Alerts on, LunarCal sends its own notifications, with three snoozes, and Calendar and Reminders still send theirs. Turn theirs off in the Settings app under Notifications → Calendar and Notifications → Reminders. Or turn LunarCal Alerts off in LunarCal's Settings to leave alerts to them.

### What is Urgent Alarm?

Mark an event or reminder Urgent Alarm and LunarCal rings an alarm for it, like Clock's, even in Silent mode or a Focus. How long each snooze lasts, and which one an alarm offers (Alarm Snooze), are set in LunarCal's Settings under Alerts.

## Widgets and Apple Watch

### How do I add LunarCal's widgets?

Touch and hold an empty part of the Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and search for LunarCal. There are three: Big Day, Day Roll and Agenda.

### Why are LunarCal's complications blank after an update?

Restart your Apple Watch: turn it off with the side button, then turn it on again. The complications come back once it has restarted.

### How do I update the weather on my watch?

It updates by itself. To fetch it straight away, open LunarCal on the watch and tap Update Weather Now under the weather.

## iCloud

### Do my lunar repeats and snoozes sync between my devices?

Yes. They're kept in your private iCloud storage, so LunarCal on your other devices has them too. LunarCal's Settings shows when they last synced, under iCloud Sync. Your events and reminders sync through Calendar and Reminders as usual.
